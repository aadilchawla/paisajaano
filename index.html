Why PAISAJAANO Matters

The Challenge

  • Limited financial literacy in rural areas
  • Difficulty in consistent savings
  • Vulnerability to predatory lending
  • Lack of banking awareness

Our Solution

  • Visual, activity-based learning
  • Local language instruction
  • Practical money management tools
  • Community-driven approach
